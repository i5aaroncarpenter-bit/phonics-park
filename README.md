# Phonics Park

Sound it out. Swing for the fences.

## How to play

- Title: Type your name, pick a jersey, tap Play Ball. Mute with the speaker button.
- Park: Pick At Bat, Defense, or Pitching, then an unlocked inning (L1-L6).
- At Bat: Hear the word, tap sound tiles in order, hit Blend to swing. Faster = extra bases. Miss = foul, retry.
- Defense and Pitching: Catch the matching sound on a fly ball, or throw the beginning sound/vowel/blend. Pass with 80 percent to unlock the next inning.
Start: node server.mjs
